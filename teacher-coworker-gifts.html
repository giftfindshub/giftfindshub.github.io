Teacher gifts have unwritten rules: keep it modest (many schools have gift limits), keep it consumable or useful, and skip anything that says "#1 Teacher" because they already own four of them. This guide follows those rules.

We focused on candles, treat baskets and small useful items with strong reviews — gifts that work for teachers, teaching assistants, coaches, nurses and coworkers alike. Nothing here needs sizing or personal taste to get right.

The thing teachers mention most in reviews and interviews isn't the gift; it's the note. Add a handwritten card from the student. It's the part they keep.
