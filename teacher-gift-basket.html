Teachers get a lot of mugs and a lot of apples. What they rarely get is a small, well-chosen basket that says someone thought about them. This one stays modest on purpose, because many schools have gift limits, and every item is something a teacher can use up or enjoy at home.

You don't need all five. Two or three of these in a small bag with a handwritten note from your child is already a generous gift. If you are organizing a class gift, the full basket splits nicely between a few families.

The note matters more than the basket. Ask your child to write one specific thing they learned or loved this year.

How to put it together

  • Pick a container: a basket, a gift box or a reusable tote all work.
  • Line the bottom with tissue paper or shredded paper so the items sit high.
  • Put the tallest item at the back and the smallest in front, then tuck in a note from your child.
  • Add a handwritten tag. It is the part people keep.
