Instead of one big toy that gets abandoned by New Year's, a themed box gives kids several things to do: build, draw, cuddle and trade. It also works well for siblings who are sharing a gift, or as a single present from grandparents.

These picks suit roughly ages 5 to 10. Check the age range on each Amazon page, especially for the building sets, and swap anything that is too young or too old for your child.

Wrap each item separately inside the box. Opening five small presents is more fun than opening one, and it stretches Christmas morning.

How to put it together

  • Pick a container: a basket, a gift box or a reusable tote all work.
  • Line the bottom with tissue paper or shredded paper so the items sit high.
  • Put the tallest item at the back and the smallest in front, then tuck in a candy cane or small treat.
  • Add a handwritten tag. It is the part people keep.
