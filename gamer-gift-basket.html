How to put it together

  • Pick a container: a basket, a gift box or a reusable tote all work.
  • Line the bottom with tissue paper or shredded paper so the items sit high.
  • Put the tallest item at the back and the smallest in front, then tuck in his favorite snack.
  • Add a handwritten tag. It is the part people keep.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put in a gamer gift basket?

A controller or headset as the main item, a charging dock, a store gift card so he can choose his own game, and something comfortable like slippers. Add snacks to finish it.
